📖 Help & Reference

Quick actions
The 3-step workflow
Step 1 — Upload filesDrop your DS1 anchor file and one or more target CSVs. The fuzzy matcher runs automatically the moment both are loaded.
Step 2 — Export for AI matchingClick "Export all for AI matching" to download a CSV pre-scored with the top 3 algorithm candidates per company. Give this to Claude and ask it to fill in the AI Decision column. Then upload Claude's completed file in the same panel.
Step 3 — Resolve remaining & exportAny companies Claude couldn't confidently match appear in the manual search panel. Search DS1 to link them, or skip them. Then export the final Excel file with DS1 IDs appended.
How the algorithm works
Exact matchCase-insensitive full name match — auto-confirmed immediately.
Fuzzy matchUses Jaro-Winkler similarity with a decision tree: first-token guard → substring check → token overlap → overall string score. Handles typos, punctuation, and legal suffix differences (Inc, LLC, Corp, etc.).
Confidence scores90%+ = near-certain. 75–89% = probable, AI confirms. Below 75% = no match sent to manual review.
Common questions
What is the anchor (DS1)?Your source of truth — typically the CRM or customer account export. Its Account ID and Intacct Customer ID get appended to every matched row in your target file.
What goes in the AI Decision column?Claude fills in CONFIRMED, UNMATCHED, or REVIEW for each company, plus the exact DS1 name it matched to in "AI Matched Anchor Name". The tool reads these on re-upload.
Can I match multiple target files at once?Yes — add as many target CSVs as you need in Step 1. The tool processes them in sequence and keeps you on Step 3 until all are exported.
What if a company truly isn't in DS1?Mark it as unmatched (Skip). It exports with a blank DS1 ID and UNMATCHED status — so you can still see it in the output.
